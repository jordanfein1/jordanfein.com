Jordan Fein is a film and commercial director known for bold visual storytelling and manifesto campaigns.

He makes pictures that move like a freight train: cinematic, unflinching, and built to break through. He’s directed Super Bowl spots, wrangled celebrities, and creates films that feel as dynamic as they are human.

His path hasn’t exactly been ordinary.

He joined a cowboy hunting club to rage in an off-the-grid desert hideaway. Cornered Spielberg to trade storytelling nightmares. Once, he walked away from a rattlesnake bite (this time).

That same restless curiosity fuels everything: The Blessing, his award-winning feature documentary that premiered on PBS, WORLD, and NHK Japan; Penn State's Super Bowl campaign; and Volvo’s “Highway Robbery,” which picked up a Clio and three Cannes Lions. His short documentary produced by People Magazine won multiple Webbys Named Independent Magazine’s Filmmaker to Watch, Jordan was a panelist at Sundance, lectured at Harvard, and is a member of the Directors Guild of America and the National Board of Review of Motion Pictures.
